Abhijeet Vishwasrao

PhD researcher · Generative AI and foundation models for physics simulation

I build generative and latent foundation models for physics simulation: diffusion and flow-matching models, JEPA latent predictors, and LLM-agent systems that run and analyze simulations on their own, to shorten engineering design cycles and speed up scientific discovery. I generate terabyte-scale datasets with open-source CFD solvers to train 100M+ parameter models on multi-GPU HPC clusters, and have published in Nature Communications, NeurIPS and the Journal of Fluid Mechanics.

PhD at the University of Michigan with Prof. Ricardo Vinuesa (expected May 2027), previously KTH as a Marie Sklodowska-Curie fellow. Collaborations with RTX, CoreWeave and PhysicsX, with Stanford, the University of Washington and Caltech, and with Argonne National Laboratory.
