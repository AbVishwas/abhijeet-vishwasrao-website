Research

Four themes, all on the same question: how to make high-fidelity physics simulation cheap enough to use inside design loops and scientific exploration, without losing the physics that matters.

Sensing and reconstruction

Diffusion priors that reconstruct full turbulent fields from a handful of sensors, and tell you where the sensors should go.

  • Diff-SPORT, Nature Communications 2026 (arXiv, code)
  • CTR Summer Program 2024, Stanford
  • Information-theoretic feature selection for spatial data, ESANN 2026

Generative flow control

Moving reward information out of the RL policy and into generative-model inference.

  • Policy-DRIFT, NeurIPS 2026 (arXiv): 49% drag reduction in channel DNS at 37x less actuation energy than DRL
  • Deep RL for uniform cooling in quenching, IJNMHFF 2024

Latent foundation models

Compact, explicit latent representations of parametrized flows, with decoders that keep the fluctuations and predictors that generalize to unseen parameters.

  • AeroJEPA, in NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo (arXiv)
  • Two-stage Reynolds-decomposition decoder for wire-coil heat-exchanger flows, Argonne 2026
  • Fast flow matching for surrogates, L4DC 2026 (arXiv)
  • Film-cooling prediction with RTX; automotive JEPA with CoreWeave and PhysicsX

Agents for simulation

LLM-agent systems that plan, run and analyze simulations on top of the latent models.

  • Agentic exploration of PDE spaces (arXiv): 1,600+ configurations, non-obvious scaling laws recovered

Tools

Nek5000 and NekRS, OpenFOAM, Cimlib, Basilisk; PyTorch and NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo; diffusion (EDM), flow matching, VAEs, JEPA, deep RL, LLM agents; multi-GPU training on HPC clusters in the EU and the US.
